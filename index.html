California Law Ensures Humans, Not AI, Are Teaching K-12 Students: Gov. Gavin Newsom signed Assembly Bill 2148 on June 30, amending the California education code to make clear that school staff and contractors in public schools are to be "natural people." The bill originally proposed sweeping restrictions on AI and education technology use in K-12 and higher education, but lawmakers scaled it back over implementation concerns, focusing instead on ensuring AI cannot replace school staff or be used to evaluate them. Author Al Muratsuchi said the law "protects educators' autonomy and ensures technology serves educators rather than replacing or penalizing them," and it takes effect January 1, 2027.

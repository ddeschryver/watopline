Oklahoma HB 3021 was signed into law by Republican Gov. Kevin Stitt on May 19 and takes effect July 1. This act, beginning with the class of students entering grade 8 in the 2025-26 school year, establishes a 23-unit curriculum for students in grades 8-12 leading to high school graduation. The curriculum permits for some credits in mathematics and laboratory science to be satisfied through successful completion of a full-time postsecondary career and technology program. Notably, the curriculum requires completion of one-half unit of personal financial literacy. The act also requires the development of a statewide catalog of business labor force-endorsed diplomas, identifying high-demand jobs and the high school coursework that prepares students for those roles

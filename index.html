This is notable. Remember 2004? Not many people do, but this was big news then, and it's still in play. The Williams Act instructional materials review is an annual compliance process in California designed to ensure that every public school student has sufficient textbooks and instructional materials to participate fully in classroom instruction. The requirement stems from the 2004 settlement of Williams v. California, a lawsuit challenging unequal educational conditions, particularly in schools serving low-income students. WV, RANDOLPH COUNTY SCHOOLS
Randolph County schools weigh staffing cuts, possible closures amid enrollment decline: Randolph County Schools has lost 239 students since October 2025, a decline expected to cut state funding by about $2 million, or roughly 30 positions. Superintendent Derek Lambert said closures may be considered and plans a community advisory committee. Hope Scholarship participants rose from 199 to 429, and the district projects a FY2028 shortfall of more than $3 million.

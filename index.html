According to district officials, the amendment adjusts revenue projections and expenditures across several funds while maintaining an ending fund balance that meets the district’s preferred reserve level of approximately 3.5 months of operating expenses. District officials noted that many of the increases were tied to state-funded programs, personnel additions and security grant funding.

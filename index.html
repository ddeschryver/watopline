Board members raised the idea of returning to more traditional models like shared classroom sets or dedicated technology labs, which promote movement and social interaction. Staff acknowledged a “national debate about screen time” and noted that rising device costs and evolving technology needs (e.g., AI infrastructure) are influencing planning decisions.
